Privacy policy.

last updated: august 2026

what we collect

When you create an account we store your email address and password (encrypted). When you build your profile we store what you enter: name, username, bio, profile picture, niches and the social links you add. If you join the waitlist we store your email.

google sign in

If you sign in with Google, Google shares your name, email address and profile picture with us. We use it only to create and log you into your YOUgc account. We never post anything to your Google account and we don't get access to your emails or files.

what's public

Your profile (name, username, picture, bio, niches, social links) and your posts are visible to everyone on the marketplace. Your email address and password are never public.

how we use your data

To run the marketplace, show your profile to brands and creators, and email you about the platform (like when the marketplace goes live). We don't sell your data. Ever.

your data, your call

You can edit your profile anytime. Want your account deleted or a copy of your data? One email to yougcmarket@gmail.com and it's handled.
