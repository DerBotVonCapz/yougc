what YOUgc is

YOUgc is a marketplace where UGC creators and brands find each other. Creators build a profile and post updates on what they create. Brands build a profile, post what they're looking for and reach out to creators directly.

your account

Keep your login safe and your profile info truthful. One account per person or brand. Fake information, impersonation, spam and other abuse of the platform result in penalties, up to removal of the account.

your content

Everything you post stays yours. By posting it on YOUgc you allow us to show it on the platform. Don't post anything you don't have the rights to, and nothing illegal, hateful or misleading.

deals and our fee

Deals are agreed directly between the creator and the brand: scope, deliverables, timeline and price. YOUgc takes no cut of your deals. The platform is free to use. We make money from optional paid features (verified, pins, the agent) and from YOUgc Studio, which is priced separately and always agreed with you before any work starts.
