questions, answered.

the short version of how YOUgc works.

?

what is YOUgc?

YOUgc

a social marketplace where brands find UGC creators by niche, see their posts, and message them directly. no agency, no middleman between you and the deal.

?

is it free to join?

YOUgc

yes. building a profile, posting and messaging are all free. the verified badge and other features are optional, for when you want more reach.

?

who is it for?

YOUgc

UGC creators who want brand deals, and brands who want real content from real people. both sides, one marketplace.

?

does YOUgc take a cut of my deals?

YOUgc

no fee on your deals today. you keep what you agree with the brand.

?

how do brands actually find me?

YOUgc

they search and scroll by niche, watch your clips and read your posts, then message you. a profile with a photo, clips and links gets messaged the most.

?

what goes on my profile?

YOUgc

a photo, a short bio, your niches, your social links, and up to 3 clips of your best work. setup takes about a minute.

?

when does the marketplace open?

YOUgc

soon. we are onboarding the first creators and brands now, so both sides are active the moment the marketplace opens. the early profiles are the ones brands see first.

?

how and when do i get paid?

YOUgc

you agree the deal directly with the brand in the DMs — what you make and what you get paid. payment happens between you and them, on your terms.

?

what does verified do?

YOUgc

the badge brands look for on your name, your posts boosted so more people see them, up to 3 posts a day instead of 2, and a profile that stands out. optional.

?

is my data safe?

YOUgc

yes. your login and private details stay private, and hard limits stop spam and mass-messaging, so nobody can blast the whole marketplace.
